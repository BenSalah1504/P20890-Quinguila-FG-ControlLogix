Project P20890 (Angola) — Complete Ladder-to-SFC Conversion

The 24 GE Fanuc VersaMax ladder routines (LAD/*.pdf) and 373 RSLogix 5000 rungs (.L5X) are decomposed into 6 cooperating IEC 61131-3 Sequential Function Charts (SFCs). Use the interactive simulator controls inside Chart 1 and Chart 3 to step through the live state transitions.

[N] Non-Stored: Action executes continuously while the step is active, and turns OFF when the step deactivates (equivalent to normal PLC COIL / OTE).
[S] Set (Stored): Latches the variable TRUE when the step becomes active; remains TRUE until explicitly reset (equivalent to SETCOIL / OTL or seal-in branch).
[R] Reset: Resets a stored variable or timer/counter to FALSE / 0 when the step becomes active (equivalent to RESETCOIL / OTU / RES).
[P] Pulse (One-Shot): Executes once on the rising edge when the step is entered (equivalent to GE POSCOIL / L5X ONS).
[L t#] Time-Limited: Runs for at most duration t# while the step is active (used for 2.0s Diesel Pump B Start/Stop pulses).
Double Bar (===) vs Single Bar (---): Double horizontal bars denote Simultaneous Parallel Branches (all active together); single bars denote Exclusive Alternative Branches.
